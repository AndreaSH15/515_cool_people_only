This is a story of 2 brave warriors against the bad coding.

Kira and David. They came down from the mountains with their laptop and a solar panel to have unlimited power against ignorance on GitHub, however,
little they know that many of their followers (students) got lost in the wild and were eaten by giant anti-technology plants, 
but only 3 strong followers survived. Together, David and Kira found the strengt to keep fighting for Github and all the repositories that were there.

Kira is the maximum authority in GitHub, we owe her all of our knowledge and her wisdom illuminates our way.
